# Artwork provenance

All three assets were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this project on 2026-11-12. The files are original generated artwork, not copied CATAN or Colonist assets. No CLI or external API key was used. Original outputs remain in the Codex generated-images folder; project files are copies without image edits.

## Assets

- `public/assets/island-hero.png` — 1672 × 941 landscape, painterly coastal island. Dark teal water gives text space on the left.
- `public/assets/terrain-atlas.png` — 1536 × 1024, six square 512 × 512 cells; rows are **wood, wheat, wool / brick, ore, desert**. No gutters. Intended for hex clipping and resource-card artwork.
- `public/assets/pieces-atlas.png` — transparent PNG, three equally spaced columns: **settlement, city, robber**. Use alpha and crop each third without distorting aspect ratio. Player-colored bases or outlines may distinguish ownership.

## Final prompts

### Island hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape hero illustration for a mature maritime strategy board game web app. Create an original richly painted island in a deep teal sea, shown from high aerial isometric perspective. The beautiful island has natural patchwork regions: dense evergreen woodland, golden wheat terraces, lush sheep grazing meadows, terracotta red clay hills, dramatic slate mountains, and a small sunlit sandy dune area. Small timber and stone coastal villages, winding roads and a few elegant sailing boats lend scale. Style is premium hand-painted historical board game illustration, relatively realistic forms with expressive brush texture and fine details, NOT photorealistic, NOT childish or flat cartoon. Warm late afternoon sunlight, misty blue atmospheric depth, copper-gold highlights, cream stone, forest green, slate blue. Wide cinematic composition with island concentrated on the right two thirds and restful dark teal water along the left for website headline overlay. Seamless natural scene, no hex grid, no text, no logos, no lettering, no borders, no interface. Landscape aspect ratio.

### Terrain atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production terrain texture atlas for a premium illustrated hex strategy board game. CRITICAL LAYOUT: a single landscape rectangular image divided into an EXACT 3-column by 2-row uniform grid, SIX equally sized SQUARE full-bleed terrain cells. Overall aspect ratio 3:2. Each cell's image must fill the entire cell edge to edge. NO gaps, NO gutters, NO borders, NO labels, NO text, NO hexagon shapes. Cell boundaries precisely at one-third and two-thirds width and half height. Read order: TOP LEFT dense forest of dark green evergreen and deciduous trees; TOP MIDDLE golden ripe wheat farmland with textured rows and softly rolling fields; TOP RIGHT lush green grass pasture with several small realistic white sheep; BOTTOM LEFT rich reddish terracotta clay hills and a small brick quarry; BOTTOM MIDDLE rugged blue-gray slate mountains with rocky crags and visible iron ore; BOTTOM RIGHT sunlit golden ochre desert sand dunes and scattered dry rocks. For all six: elevated near-top-down aerial view without any horizon or sky, landscape terrain extends to every edge. Mature realistic hand-painted gouache/oil concept-art style, finely detailed material textures, rich natural colors, harmonious warm sunlight from upper left. Like a beautifully painted historical atlas, not a photo, not pale cartoon. These cells will be clipped into individual hexagons in code; keep focal terrain in cell center and natural matching surrounding terrain to all edges. No buildings, no UI, no game pieces, no watermarks.

### Piece atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: TRANSPARENT game piece sprite atlas, premium mature illustrated strategy board game. Exact layout: ONE ROW of THREE equal square cells in a 3:1 wide image. Within each cell center one isolated object, equal scale, ample transparent margin, no ground scene. LEFT cell: small medieval settlement miniature, one rustic white-plaster timber cottage with a muted terracotta roof. CENTER cell: larger medieval city miniature, compact cream-stone cluster with a crenellated tower and several copper-red roofs. RIGHT cell: dark charcoal hooded robber pawn miniature with simple cloak silhouette and small bronze belt, no weapons, no face. All three rendered in matching elevated three-quarter isometric view, premium hand-painted semi-realistic board game illustration with carved wood and stone texture, rich warm muted colors and restrained fine details. Objects should be readable when tiny. True transparent alpha background throughout, no checkerboard, no background color, no floor, no text, no labels, no borders, no panels, no watermark. Keep every object completely inside its own equal-width third of the atlas.
